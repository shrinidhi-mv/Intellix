# Wheat Yellow / Stripe Rust (Puccinia striiformis) Management

**Crop:** Wheat (Triticum aestivum / Gehun)
**Category:** Fungal Disease
**Local Names:** पीला रतुआ (Hindi), மஞ்சள் துரு நோய் (Tamil), పసుపు కుంకుమ తెగులు (Telugu)

## Symptoms and Identification
1. **Linear Yellow Streaks:** Characterized by bright yellow, powdery pustules arranged in distinct narrow parallel stripes or rows along the leaf veins.
2. **Dusting on Touch:** Touching affected leaves leaves yellow powdery spores (urediniospores) on fingers or clothing.
3. **Severe Infestation:** Leaves turn chlorotic and brown, dry up prematurely, resulting in shriveled kernels and up to 50-80% yield loss in susceptible varieties.

## Favorable Conditions
- Cool weather: Day temperatures between 10-20°C and night temperatures 5-10°C accompanied by high humidity, fog, or frequent dew.
- High nitrogen fertilizer application with delayed sowing.

## Chemical Treatment
- **At Initial Patch Appearance:** Spray **Propiconazole 25% EC (Tilt)** @ 1.0 ml/liter of water (200 ml per acre dissolved in 200 liters of water).
- Alternative: **Tebuconazole 25.9% EC** @ 1.0 ml/liter of water or **Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC** @ 1 ml/liter.
- Repeat the spray after 15 days if cloudy weather persists and new active yellow lesions are observed.

## Preventative and Cultural Measures
- Grow resistant cultivars recommended for northern and central plains: **HD-3086, HD-2967, DBW-187 (Karan Vandana), DBW-222**.
- Inspect field borders regularly between December and February, especially in shady corners.
- Avoid late sowing; sow timely between November 5 to November 25.

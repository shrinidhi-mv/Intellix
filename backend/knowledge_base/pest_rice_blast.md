# Rice Blast Disease (Pyricularia oryzae) Management

**Crop:** Rice (Oryza sativa / Paddy)
**Category:** Fungal Disease
**Local Names:** குலை நோய் (Tamil), झुलसा / ब्लास्ट रोग (Hindi), అగ్గితెగులు (Telugu), ಬೆಂಕಿ ರೋಗ (Kannada)

## Symptoms and Identification
1. **Leaf Blast:** Diamond or spindle-shaped lesions with greyish or white centers and brown to reddish-brown margins. In severe cases, multiple spots coalesce causing complete drying of leaves.
2. **Node Blast:** Blackened nodes; the infected culm breaks easily at the node.
3. **Neck Blast:** Greyish-black lesion at the panicle base (neck). The neck rots, causing the panicle to droop and break, leading to empty (chaffy) grains.

## Favorable Conditions
- High relative humidity (>90%), prolonged leaf wetness (dew or light rains).
- Night temperatures around 20-24°C.
- Excessive use of nitrogenous fertilizers (Urea) and close plant spacing.

## Recommended Chemical Control
- **Foliar Spray:** Spray **Tricyclazole 75% WP** @ 0.6 g/liter of water (120 g per acre in 200 liters of water) at the initial appearance of spots.
- Alternative fungicide: **Isoprothiolane 40% EC** @ 1.5 ml/liter or **Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC** @ 1 ml/liter.
- For neck blast prevention, apply one protective spray at early boot leaf stage and a second spray at 5-10% flowering stage.

## Cultural and Preventative Measures
- Treat seeds prior to sowing with **Carbendazim 50% WP** or **Tricyclazole** @ 2 g/kg seed.
- Avoid excessive nitrogen; apply nitrogen in 3-4 split doses rather than a single heavy basal dose.
- Plant resistant or tolerant varieties recommended by state universities (e.g., IR 64, CO 47, ADT 43).
- Maintain proper water drainage to reduce field humidity during overcast days.

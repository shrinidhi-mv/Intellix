# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY) Crop Insurance

**Category:** Government Agricultural Insurance Scheme
**Objective:** Financial support and risk coverage for farmers suffering crop loss/damage arising from unforeseen weather perils, pests, and natural calamities.

## Farmer Premium Rates
The government heavily subsidizes crop insurance premiums. Farmers only pay a very low fixed premium rate:
- **Kharif Food & Oilseed Crops (Paddy, Cotton, Maize, Pulses):** Only **2.0%** of the sum insured.
- **Rabi Food & Oilseed Crops (Wheat, Mustard, Barley, Gram):** Only **1.5%** of the sum insured.
- **Commercial and Annual Horticultural Crops (Sugarcane, Vegetables, Spices, Fruits):** Only **5.0%** of the sum insured.
- The balance of the actuarial premium is shared equally (50:50) between the Central and State Governments.

## Coverage of Risks
1. **Prevented Sowing / Planting Risk:** If widespread drought or adverse weather prevents planting/sowing in the notified area (up to 25% of sum insured paid).
2. **Standing Crop Loss (Sowing to Harvesting):** Yield losses due to non-preventable risks like flood, inundation, drought, dry spells, landslides, hailstorm, cyclone, natural fire, pests and diseases.
3. **Post-Harvest Losses:** Coverage available for up to a maximum period of **14 days** from harvesting for crops kept in "cut and spread" condition in the field for drying against cyclone, unseasonal rains, and hailstorm.
4. **Localized Calamities:** Loss or damage resulting from localized hail, landslide, cloudburst, and inundation affecting isolated farms.

## Critical Claim Intimation Rule
- In case of localized calamities or post-harvest loss, the farmer **MUST report the crop loss within 72 hours** of the incident.
- Report channels:
  - Through the **Crop Insurance App** (Farmer corner),
  - Calling the National Crop Insurance Helpline: 14447,
  - Notifying the concerned local bank branch, agriculture officer, or insurance company representative.

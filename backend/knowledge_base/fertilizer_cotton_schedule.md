# Cotton Fertilizer Schedule and Nutrient Management

**Crop:** Hybrid / Bt Cotton (Kapas / பருத்தி)
**Recommended Dose of Fertilizers (RDF):** 120:60:60 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare (approx. 50:25:25 kg per acre) for irrigated hybrid cotton.

## Stage-wise Fertilizer Timetable

| Growth Stage | Timing (Days After Sowing) | Application per acre | Nutrient Supplied |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basal Application** (At Sowing / Furrows) | Day 0 | - DAP: 55 kg<br>- MOP (Potash): 15 kg<br>- Urea: 25 kg<br>- Micronutrient Zinc Sulphate: 10 kg | Full Phosphorus, 33% Potassium, 20% Nitrogen |
| **Square Formation Stage** | 30 - 35 DAS | - Urea: 35 kg<br>- MOP: 15 kg | 30% Nitrogen, 33% Potassium |
| **Flowering Stage** | 55 - 60 DAS | - Urea: 35 kg<br>- MOP: 15 kg | 30% Nitrogen, Remaining Potassium |
| **Boll Development Stage** | 80 - 85 DAS | - Urea: 20 kg | Remaining 20% Nitrogen |

## Foliar Sprays for Square & Boll Retention
- **Prevent Square & Boll Dropping:** Spray **Planofix (Alpha Naphthyl Acetic Acid 4.5% SL)** @ 0.25 ml per liter of water (50 ml in 200 L water) at square formation and peak flowering stage.
- **Magnesium Deficiency (Reddening of Cotton Leaves):** If mature bottom leaves turn reddish-purple while veins remain green:
  - Foliar Spray: Spray **Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO4)** @ 10 g/liter of water (1%) along with 10 g Urea/liter at 60 and 80 DAS.
- **Boron Micronutrient:** Foliar spray of **Solubor (Boron 20%)** @ 1 g/liter of water at peak flowering improves pollen viability, boll size, and prevents internal boll rot.

# Sugarcane Fertilizer Application Schedule

**Crop:** Sugarcane (Ganna / கரும்பு)
**Recommended Dose of Fertilizers (RDF):** 250:115:115 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare (approx. 100:45:45 kg per acre) for plant crop in tropical and sub-tropical conditions.

## Stage-wise Fertilizer Timetable

| Timing / Stage | Days After Planting (DAP) | Recommended Application per acre | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basal Application** (Furrow placement) | Day 0 (At Planting) | - Single Super Phosphate (SSP): 280 kg (or DAP 100 kg)<br>- Muriate of Potash (MOP): 25 kg<br>- Urea: 45 kg<br>- Ferrous Sulphate: 10 kg (if calcareous soil) | Full Phosphorus, 33% Potash, 20% Nitrogen |
| **First Top Dressing (Tillering Start)** | 30 - 35 DAP | - Urea: 45 kg | Tillering initiation |
| **Second Top Dressing (Peak Tillering)** | 60 DAP | - Urea: 45 kg<br>- MOP: 25 kg | Shoot multiplication and girth |
| **Third Top Dressing (Final Earthing Up)** | 90 - 100 DAP | - Urea: 45 kg<br>- MOP: 25 kg | Grand growth elongation; complete Nitrogen before 120 days |

## Critical Guidelines for Sugarcane Nutrition
- **Complete Nitrogen Application Early:** All nitrogen fertilizers MUST be applied before 90-100 days (maximum 120 days). Nitrogen applied beyond 120 days leads to water shoots, late vegetative flushes, reduced sucrose content (Brix), and excessive pest incidence.
- **Earthing Up Integration:** Perform partial earthing up at 60 DAP and final earthing up at 90-100 DAP immediately after fertilizer top dressing to incorporate nutrients into the root zone and prevent lodging.
- **Biofertilizers:** Inoculate setts with **Azotobacter / Azospirillum** @ 5 kg/ha and **Phosphobacteria (PSB)** @ 5 kg/ha mixed with 500 kg FYM to save 20-25% chemical nitrogen and phosphorus requirements.

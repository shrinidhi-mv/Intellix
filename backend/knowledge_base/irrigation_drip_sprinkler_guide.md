# Drip and Sprinkler Micro-Irrigation Guidance

**Category:** Water Management & Micro-Irrigation Systems
**Applicability:** Row crops (Cotton, Sugarcane, Vegetables, Orchards) and closely spaced crops (Wheat, Pulses, Oilseeds).

## Drip Irrigation System Management
1. **Water Savings & Efficiency:** Drip systems deliver water directly to the plant root zone with 90-95% water use efficiency, saving 40-60% water compared to surface flood irrigation and reducing weed growth between rows.
2. **Operating Pressure:** Maintain system operating pressure at **1.0 to 1.5 kg/cm²** (14-21 psi) at the field sub-main manifold.
3. **Emitter Discharge Rates:**
   - Sugarcane / Cotton: In-line drip tubing with dripper discharge of **2.0 or 4.0 liters per hour (lph)** spaced at 40 cm to 60 cm intervals.
   - Run time: 1.5 to 2.5 hours every alternate day depending on soil type and evapotranspiration rates.
4. **Maintenance & Acid Treatment:**
   - **Filter Cleaning:** Flush sand media filters and disc/screen filters weekly.
   - **Acid Washing for Clogged Emitters:** If emitters clog due to calcium/magnesium salts (white crust), inject **Hydrochloric Acid (HCl 33%)** or **Nitric Acid** to maintain pH 4.0 in the system for 10-15 minutes, allow to stand for 24 hours, and then flush sub-mains and laterals.
   - **Chlorination for Algal Slime:** Inject bleaching powder (Calcium Hypochlorite) @ 20 ppm to dissolve bacterial and algal biofilm in drip lines.

## Sprinkler Irrigation Guidance
1. **Suitable Crops:** Wheat, groundnut, mustard, pulses, and forage crops.
2. **Operating Pressure:** Operates at **2.5 to 3.5 kg/cm²** (35-50 psi).
3. **Spacing & Overlap:** Common spacing is 12m x 12m or 10m x 10m. To achieve uniform water distribution (Christiansen Uniformity Coefficient >85%), nozzles must provide 50-60% sprinkler spray overlap.
4. **Wind Caution:** Avoid operating overhead sprinklers during high wind conditions (>10-12 km/h) as wind distorts droplet distribution patterns and causes dry spots.

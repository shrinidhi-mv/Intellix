# Fall Armyworm (Spodoptera frugiperda) in Maize

**Crop:** Maize / Corn (Makka)
**Category:** Invasive Insect Pest
**Local Names:** படைப்புழு (Tamil), फॉल आर्मीवर्म / लश्करी सुंडी (Hindi), కత్తెర పురుగు (Telugu)

## Symptoms and Damage
1. **Window-paning:** Young larvae scrape the green epidermal leaf tissue, creating semi-transparent elongated "windows" on tender leaves.
2. **Shot-holes & Ragged Leaves:** As larvae grow (3rd-6th instar), they chew large irregular holes in the whorl. Leaves emerging from the whorl look severely shredded and ragged.
3. **Frass Accumulation:** Large amounts of saw-dust-like brownish larval excreta (frass) accumulate inside the central whorl of the corn plant.
4. **Larval Identification:** Larvae have an inverted 'Y' shaped mark on the dark head capsule and four elevated black pinacula dots forming a square on the 8th abdominal segment.

## Chemical Control & Dosages
- **Early Whorl Stage (First 15-20 days):** Spray **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC (Coragen)** @ 0.4 ml/liter of water (80 ml per acre in 200 L water) directly into the central whorl using a knapsack sprayer with nozzle directed downwards.
- **Mid-stage (30-45 days):** Spray **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG** @ 0.4 g/liter of water (80 g/acre) OR **Spinetoram 11.7% SC** @ 0.5 ml/liter of water (100 ml/acre).
- **Poison Baiting (for older caterpillars):** Mix 10 kg rice bran + 1 kg jaggery in 2-3 liters of water, ferment for 24 hours, then mix 100 g Thiodicarb 75% WP. Apply small pellets into the plant whorls in late evening.

## Biological & Cultural Control
- Release egg parasitoid **Trichogramma pretiosum** @ 50,000/acre at weekly intervals.
- Apply neem-based formulation **Azadirachtin 1500 ppm** @ 5 ml/liter as first line defense.
- Install pheromone traps @ 5 traps/acre for adult moth monitoring.

# Wheat Fertilizer Application Schedule and Nutrient Management

**Crop:** Wheat (Gehun / கோதுமை)
**Recommended Dose of Fertilizers (RDF):** 120:60:40 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare (approx. 50:25:16 kg per acre) under timely-sown irrigated conditions.

## Stage-wise Fertilizer Timetable

| Growth Stage | Days After Sowing (DAS) | Recommended Fertilizer (per acre) | Key Nutrient |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basal Application** (At Sowing / Drilling) | Day 0 | - Diammonium Phosphate (DAP): 55 kg<br>- Muriate of Potash (MOP): 27 kg<br>- Urea: 25 kg | Full Phosphorus, Full Potassium, 1/3rd of Nitrogen |
| **Crown Root Initiation (CRI)** | 20 - 25 DAS (1st Irrigation) | - Urea: 40 kg (broadcast just before or immediately after 1st watering) | 1/3rd of Nitrogen |
| **Late Tillering / Jointing Stage** | 40 - 45 DAS (2nd Irrigation) | - Urea: 40 kg | Remaining 1/3rd Nitrogen |

## Micronutrients and Foliar Boosters
- **Sulphur Application:** In light soils or areas deficient in sulphur, apply **Bentonite Sulphur 90%** @ 10 kg/acre at the time of sowing to increase grain protein content and gluten strength.
- **Manganese Deficiency:** Common in wheat grown after paddy in sandy loam soils; leaves show greyish-brown specks between veins.
  - Foliar Spray: Spray **0.5% Manganese Sulphate** (1 kg in 200 L water) 2-3 days before the first irrigation, followed by a second spray at 10 days after irrigation.
- **Terminal Heat Management:** Spray **Potassium Nitrate (13:0:45)** @ 1% (2 kg in 200 L water) at boot leaf and flowering stage to protect grains from sudden heat waves in March.

## Best Application Practices
- Always place phosphatic fertilizers (DAP/SSP) 4-5 cm below the seed using seed-cum-fertilizer drills for maximum root uptake.
- Do not apply nitrogen top dressing after flowering/heading stage, as it prolongs vegetative growth and induces crop lodging.

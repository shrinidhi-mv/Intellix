# Soil Health Card (SHC) Scheme

**Category:** Central Government Soil Health & Nutrient Stewardship Initiative
**Objective:** Assist farmers to improve crop productivity through balanced, soil test-based fertilizer and manure application.

## Soil Parameters Tested
The Soil Health Card provides a 12-parameter diagnostic report of the farmer's soil:
1. **Primary Nutrients (3):** Available Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K).
2. **Secondary Nutrients (1):** Sulphur (S).
3. **Micronutrients (5):** Zinc (Zn), Iron (Fe), Copper (Cu), Manganese (Mn), Boron (B).
4. **Physical Parameters (3):** pH (soil acidity/alkalinity), Electrical Conductivity (EC - soil salinity), and Organic Carbon (OC %).

## Card Cycle and Crop Recommendations
- Issued once every **3 years** for an agricultural holding after testing composite soil samples collected using GPS coordinates (grid of 2.5 ha in irrigated and 10 ha in rainfed areas).
- **Customized Fertilizer Recommendations:** Recommends exact quantities of chemical fertilizers (Urea, DAP, MOP) and organic manures needed for 6 chosen crops to prevent wasteful over-fertilization and save input costs (typically saves 15-20% on urea/DAP costs).

## How Farmers Can Get Tested
- Soil samples are collected by agriculture extension officers or through local **Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVK)** and village-level Soil Testing Laboratories.
- Results and digital soil health cards can be downloaded online with the farmer's mobile number or Aadhaar from `soilhealth.dac.gov.in`.
- Corrective Amendments: If soil pH is below 6.5 (acidic), the card recommends Agricultural Lime (CaCO3); if pH is above 8.5 (alkaline/sodic), it recommends Gypsum application.

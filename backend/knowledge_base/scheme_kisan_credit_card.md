# Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme

**Category:** Institutional Agricultural Credit & Working Capital Scheme
**Target Beneficiaries:** Individual or joint farmer-cultivators, tenant farmers, oral lessees, sharecroppers, and Self Help Groups (SHGs). Also extended to animal husbandry, poultry, and fisheries.

## Key Financial Benefits
1. **Subsidized Interest Rate (4% Effective Rate):**
   - Normal benchmark interest rate is 9%.
   - Government of India provides a **2% Interest Subvention**.
   - Additional **3% Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI)** if the farmer repays the loan on or before the due date.
   - Net effective interest rate is therefore only **4.0% per annum** for loans up to ₹3,00,000 (3 Lakhs).
2. **Collateral-Free Loan Limit:**
   - Agricultural loans up to **₹1,60,000 (1.6 Lakhs)** are granted without needing any land mortgage or collateral security.
   - For tie-up arrangements with processing factories (e.g. sugar mills), collateral-free limit is extended up to ₹3,00,000.

## Usage and Flexibility
- The card works as a revolving cash credit facility. Farmers can withdraw cash at ATMs, point-of-sale (PoS) machines at input dealers, or bank branches as and when needed during the crop season.
- Interest is charged only on the actual amount withdrawn and for the exact number of days borrowed.
- Validity: 5 years, subject to annual review.

## Application Documents Needed
- Simplified 1-page KCC application form.
- Identity and Address Proof: Aadhaar Card / Voter ID / Driving License.
- Land ownership document: RoR (Record of Rights / Patta / 7/12 extract / Khatauni) or lease agreement copy.
- Banks are instructed to process and sanction eligible KCC applications within **14 days** of receipt.

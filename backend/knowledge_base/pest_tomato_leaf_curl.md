# Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (ToLCV) and Whitefly Management

**Crop:** Tomato (Solanum lycopersicum)
**Category:** Viral Disease (Transmitted by Whitefly *Bemisia tabaci*)
**Local Names:** இலை சுருள் நோய் (Tamil), पर्ण कुंचन रोग / पत्ता मरोड़ (Hindi), ఆకు ముడత తెగులు (Telugu)

## Symptoms and Identification
1. **Severe Stunting:** Infected tomato plants show marked dwarfing with shortened internodes, giving a bushy or rosette appearance.
2. **Upward & Downward Leaf Curling:** Leaves curl upwards or downwards, become thicker, leathery, and brittle.
3. **Vein Clearing & Chlorosis:** Yellowing of interveinal leaf margins with reduced leaf lamina size.
4. **Poor Fruit Set:** Severely affected plants drop flowers and produce either no fruit or very few, small, deformed, and unmarketable fruits.

## Vector Biology
- The virus is transmitted efficiently by the silverleaf whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*). The disease is NOT seed-borne and does not transmit mechanically by touch. Controlling the whitefly vector is essential to controlling the disease.

## Whitefly Vector & Disease Management
- **Nursery Protection:** Raise tomato seedlings under 40-50 mesh insect-proof nylon net covers for the first 25-30 days to prevent early whitefly feeding.
- **Yellow Sticky Traps:** Erect bright yellow sticky sheets @ 15-20 traps/acre at crop canopy height to capture adult whiteflies.
- **Chemical Vector Sprays (Alternate modes of action):**
  - **Diafenthiuron 50% WP** @ 1.25 g/liter of water (250 g/acre).
  - **Spiromesifen 22.9% SC** @ 1.25 ml/liter of water (250 ml/acre).
  - **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL** @ 0.5 ml/liter of water (100 ml/acre) or **Thiamethoxam 25% WG** @ 0.4 g/liter of water.
  - Spray on the undersides of leaves where whitefly nymphs and adults congregate.
- **Botanical Spray:** Spray **Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%)** or Neem oil 1500 ppm @ 3 ml/liter at weekly intervals during vegetative phase.
- Uproot and bury infected plants during early vegetative stage to prevent field spread.

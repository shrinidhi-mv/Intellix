# Rice (Paddy) Fertilizer Application Schedule and Nutrient Management

**Crop:** Rice / Paddy (Nel / Dhan)
**Recommended Dose of Fertilizers (RDF):** 120:50:50 kg N:P2O5:K2O per hectare (approx. 50:20:20 kg per acre) for high-yielding medium duration varieties.

## Stage-wise Fertilizer Application Timetable

| Growth Stage / Timing | Days After Transplanting (DAT) | Fertilizer Application (per acre) | Nutrient Supplied |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basal Application** (Final Puddling) | Day 0 (At Transplanting) | - DAP: 45 kg (or SSP 125 kg)<br>- Muriate of Potash (MOP): 17 kg<br>- Urea: 22 kg<br>- Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4 21%): 10 kg | Full Phosphorus (P), 50% Potash (K), 25% Nitrogen (N), Micronutrient Zinc |
| **Active Tillering Stage** | 20 - 25 DAT | - Urea: 33 kg<br>- Neem Coated Urea preferred | 35% Nitrogen (N) |
| **Panicle Initiation (PI) Stage** | 40 - 45 DAT | - Urea: 22 kg<br>- MOP: 17 kg | 25% Nitrogen (N), Remaining 50% Potash (K) |
| **Booting / Heading Stage** | 60 - 65 DAT (Optional) | - Urea: 11 kg (only if leaves turn pale green) | 15% Nitrogen (N) |

## Crucial Micronutrient Guidelines
- **Zinc Deficiency (Khaira Disease):** If leaves develop reddish-brown pigmentation or rusty spots, zinc is deficient.
  - Foliar Spray: Spray **0.5% Zinc Sulphate** (5 g/L) + **0.25% Agricultural Lime** (2.5 g/L) dissolved in water, 2 sprays at 10-day intervals.
- **Do not mix Zinc Sulphate directly with DAP or Single Super Phosphate (SSP)** in the soil, as phosphorus chemically locks zinc into insoluble zinc phosphate. Apply zinc sulphate 3-4 days after basal phosphate application.

## Water Management during Fertilizer Application
- Drain excess standing water from the paddy field before broadcasting urea; keep only a thin water film (1-2 cm).
- Re-irrigate 24 to 48 hours after fertilizer application to allow nitrogen absorption into the soil root zone.

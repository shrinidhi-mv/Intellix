# Critical Moisture Stress Stages for Major Crops

**Category:** Irrigation Scheduling & Water Deficit Management

Water stress at critical growth stages causes irreversible yield reductions. If irrigation water is scarce or limited, water applications MUST be prioritized during these physiological windows.

## Crop-by-Crop Critical Irrigation Windows

### 1. Rice (Paddy)
- **Most Critical Stages:**
  - **Panicle Initiation to Flowering (Booting & Anthesis):** Water stress during this 15-day window causes spikelet sterility and massive chaffy grain formation (yield loss up to 50%).
  - **Tillering Stage:** Adequate shallow water (2-3 cm) encourages maximum tillers.
- *Water Saving Technique:* Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) — allow field water to drop 15 cm below soil surface in an observation field tube before re-irrigating during vegetative phase, but maintain continuous shallow submergence from flowering until milk stage.

### 2. Wheat
- **Number of Irrigations Available:**
  - **If only 1 irrigation is available:** Apply at **Crown Root Initiation (CRI)** (20-25 days after sowing). Missing this causes severe yield loss of 35-40%.
  - **If 2 irrigations are available:** Apply at CRI (20-25 DAS) and **Booting / Heading stage** (75-80 DAS).
  - **If 3 irrigations are available:** Apply at CRI, Late Tillering (40-45 DAS), and **Milking / Grain Filling stage** (100-105 DAS).
  - **If 4-6 irrigations are available:** CRI (21 DAS), Tillering (42 DAS), Late Jointing (65 DAS), Flowering (85 DAS), Milk stage (105 DAS), and Dough stage (120 DAS).

### 3. Cotton
- **Critical Stages:**
  - **Square Initiation (45-50 DAS):** Moderate moisture needed; avoid waterlogging.
  - **Flowering and Peak Boll Formation (70-110 DAS):** Most critical! Water stress causes massive shedding of floral buds (squares) and young green bolls.
- *Warning:* Avoid heavy watering close to boll burst / maturity as it causes boll rot and delays harvest.

### 4. Maize (Corn)
- **Critical Stages:**
  - **Tasseling and Silking (Flowering stage, 45-60 DAS):** Most vulnerable to moisture stress. Drought for even 2-3 days during silking desensitizes silks, hinders pollination, and leads to poorly filled, barren cobs.
  - **Early Grain Filling Stage:** Moisture stress leads to shriveled light kernels.

### 5. Sugarcane
- **Formative Stage (60 to 130 days after planting):** This is the tillering and stalk initiation phase where 70% of crop water requirement is needed. Irrigation interval should be 7-10 days in sandy loam soils.

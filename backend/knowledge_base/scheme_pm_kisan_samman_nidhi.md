# Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)

**Category:** Central Sector Government Welfare Scheme
**Target Beneficiaries:** All landholding farmer families across India.

## Scheme Overview and Financial Assistance
- **Financial Benefit:** An income support of **₹6,000 per year** provided to eligible farmer families.
- **Installment Schedule:** Released directly into bank accounts via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in **three equal four-monthly installments of ₹2,000 each**:
  - Period 1: April to July
  - Period 2: August to November
  - Period 3: December to March

## Mandatory Eligibility and Verification Requirements
1. **Land Ownership Records:** The landholder must possess cultivable agricultural land registered in their name (Land Record Seeding).
2. **Mandatory Aadhaar e-KYC:** Aadhaar-based OTP verification or biometric authentication at Common Service Centers (CSC) / State Agri offices is strictly required to receive payments.
3. **Aadhaar-Bank Account Seeding:** Bank account must be mapped to the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) Aadhaar Payment Bridge (APB).

## Key Exclusions
- Institutional landholders.
- Farmer families holding constitutional posts, former or current Ministers, MPs, MLAs, Mayors, or Panchayat Chairpersons.
- Serving or retired officers and employees of Central/State Government ministries, public sector enterprises, or autonomous bodies (excluding Multi Tasking Staff / Class IV / Group D employees).
- All persons who paid Income Tax in the last assessment year.
- Professionals like Doctors, Engineers, Lawyers, Chartered Accountants, and Architects.

## How to Check Status & Resolve Issues
- Farmers can verify payment status, update Aadhaar details, or complete e-KYC online at the official portal: `pmkisan.gov.in` under the "Farmer's Corner" or via the PM-KISAN mobile app with Face Authentication.
- Toll-free Helpline: 155261 / 1800115526 / 011-24300606.

# Sugarcane Red Rot (Colletotrichum falcatum) Control

**Crop:** Sugarcane (Saccharum officinarum / Ganna)
**Category:** Fungal Disease
**Local Names:** செவ்வழுகல் நோய் (Tamil), लाल सड़न रोग (Hindi), ఎర్ర కుళ్ళు తెగులు (Telugu)

## Symptoms and Identification
1. **Third / Fourth Leaf Drying:** Initial symptoms appear as loss of green color and yellowing of the third or fourth leaf from the top, gradually withering downwards.
2. **Internal Stalk Discoloration:** Splitting the infected cane lengthwise reveals a longitudinal dark red discoloration of the pith interrupted by distinct white cross-bands or transverse patches (pathognomonic symptom).
3. **Alcoholic Odor:** The internal infected tissue emits an unpleasant sour or acidic fermented alcoholic smell.
4. **Cavity Formation:** Pith dries up forming longitudinal cavities filled with greyish-white mycelial growth.

## Spread & Source of Infection
- Primary source: Infected seed setts used for planting.
- Secondary source: Irrigation water, flood runoff, and cultivation implements carrying fungal spores.

## Management and Control Measures
- **Seed Sett Treatment:** Dip seed setts before planting for 15 minutes in a solution of **Carbendazim 50% WP** @ 1.0 g/liter of water (or 100 g in 100 L water) or **Thiophanate-Methyl 70% WP** @ 1.5 g/liter.
- **Moist Hot Air Treatment (MHAT):** Treat cane setts at 54°C for 2 hours to eliminate internal set-borne mycelium.
- **Resistant Varieties:** Plant certified disease-free setts of resistant/tolerant varieties such as **Co 0238 (where tolerant), Co 86032, Co 0118, Co 98014**.
- **Field Sanitation:** Rogue out and burn affected clumps immediately along with roots. Do not use irrigation channels that run through infected plots into healthy fields.
- Avoid ratooning infected sugarcane crops.

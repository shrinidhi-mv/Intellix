# Cotton Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella) Control

**Crop:** Cotton (Kapas)
**Category:** Insect Pest
**Local Names:** இளஞ்சிவப்பு காய்ப்புழு (Tamil), गुलाबी सुंडी (Hindi), గులాబీ రంగు కాయ తొలుచు పురుగు (Telugu)

## Symptoms and Damage Assessment
1. **Rosetted Flowers:** Larvae web the petals together into an unopened rosette shape; infested flowers fail to open normally.
2. **Boll Entry:** Young caterpillars bore into green bolls, leaving tiny pinhole entry points that seal over quickly. Inside the boll, the larva feeds on developing seeds, lint, and locules.
3. **Lint Staining:** Infested lint becomes discolored, stained brown/yellow, and loses fiber strength and market value. Premature boll dropping occurs.

## Economic Threshold Level (ETL)
- 8 pink bollworm moths trapped per pheromone trap per night for 3 consecutive nights, OR
- 10% damaged green bolls or rosetted flowers (sample 20 green bolls from the field).

## Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Chemical Control
- **Pheromone Trapping:** Install **Pheromone traps with Gossyplure septa** @ 5 traps/acre at 45 days after sowing (DAS) for monitoring, and 8-10 traps/acre for mass trapping. Replace lures every 25-30 days.
- **Botanical Spray (Initial Stage):** Spray **Neem Oil 1500 ppm** @ 3-5 ml/liter of water or 5% Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE) at squaring stage to deter egg laying.
- **Targeted Chemical Spray (Only when ETL is crossed):**
  - **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG** @ 0.4 g/liter of water (80-100 g/acre in 200 L water).
  - Or **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC** @ 0.3 ml/liter of water (60 ml/acre).
  - Or **Profenofos 50% EC** @ 2 ml/liter of water (400 ml/acre) for late-stage boll opening.

## Cultural Practices
- Strictly avoid ratooning of cotton as it carries over pests to the next season.
- Complete harvest by December-January and shred stalks immediately.
- Deep summer ploughing to expose pupae to predatory birds and solar heat.
